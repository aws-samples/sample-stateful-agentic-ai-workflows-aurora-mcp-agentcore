# Meridian travel photography

Critical showcase imagery is stored locally so the stage experience does not
depend on third-party image delivery.

- `tuscany-vineyard.jpg`: "Vineyard in Chianti Tuscany" by Jason Parrish,
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vineyard_in_Chianti_Tuscany.jpg),
  licensed under [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).
- `recovery-flight.jpg`: "ANA 777 landing at NRT" by Angelo DeSantis,
  [Wikimedia Commons](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ANA_777_landing_at_NRT_(7185678977)_(2).jpg>),
  licensed under [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).
- Other stage-critical Tokyo, recovery, city, beach, wellness, and adventure
  JPEGs are user-supplied generated editorial images installed on August 15,
  2026. They are intentionally photorealistic but must not be described as
  documentary photography.
- `catalog/CTY-004.jpg` and `catalog/BCH-003.jpg` retain the earlier catalog
  photography.
- `catalog/*.jpg` uses stable package-ID paths, keeping projected cards
  reliable without venue Wi-Fi. A curated replacement can overwrite the
  matching `catalog/<package_id>.jpg` path without a code change.
- `SHOWCASE_IMAGE_MANIFEST.md` records the intended composition and provenance
  contract for every presenter-query asset.
